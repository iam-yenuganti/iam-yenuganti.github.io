I've spent 20+ years building and securing enterprise platforms — mostly on Azure, mostly in healthcare. These days that means cloud architecture and landing zones, DevSecOps pipelines, Zero Trust identity and network design, and the compliance work that comes with handling health data.

Before the cloud work I ran data centres, mail platforms, and infrastructure teams, which is still how I think about most problems: what breaks, who gets paged, and how you prove it was handled.

I write up the useful parts on the blog — practical architecture notes, and the mistakes worth avoiding.
